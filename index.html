THE CASE FILE · READ FIRST

The night the clocks disagreed

At 11:47 PM, Dr. Ira Sen disappeared from Lab 3 of the Astra Time Institute. The magnetic lock registered no opening. The corridor camera shows an empty hallway. Yet four people independently reported seeing Ira leave the building.

Your mission

Reconstruct who was where and when. Identify which record was altered. Determine how the locked-room disappearance was staged. Extract the six-letter instruction hidden across the puzzles.

Case rule: Keep a scratchpad. Some answers become evidence in later puzzles. Don't treat a timestamp as a fact simply because it is printed neatly.

PUZZLE 03 · DEDUCTION

Four witnesses, four alibis

Each person was in a different location at a different time and handled a different object. Complete the grid using the clues. Times: 11:42, 11:45, 11:47, 11:50. Locations: Archive, Lab, Control Room, Library. Objects: Brass key, calibration wand, red folder, access card.

Clues

  1. The person in the Archive was there at 11:42 and carried the red folder.
  2. Arjun was in the Lab, but not at 11:47.
  3. Riya was not in the Library and did not carry the access card.
  4. Lila arrived three minutes after the person in the Control Room.
  5. Malhotra was either in the Library or carrying the brass key.
  6. The calibration wand was in the Lab.
  7. The 11:47 appointment was in the Control Room.
  8. Riya was seen at 11:42 or 11:50.

Clues 1, 2, 6 and 7 fix the Archive, Lab and Control Room slots. The three-minute relation then pins down the remaining time.

A message out of time

Decode the plaintext. Then use the system override from Puzzle 02 as a key: shift each plaintext letter forward by the sum of the four override digits, reduced modulo 26. A=0, B=1 … Z=25.

PUZZLE 07 · FORENSIC OBSERVATION

The lab, before and after

Compare the before-and-after laboratory scenes in the cover artwork. Identify ten changes and classify each as moved, missing, added or altered. One apparent change is a camera-angle decoy.

PUZZLE 08 · TIMELINE RECONSTRUCTION

The 11:47 contradiction

CCTV has a seven-minute gap beginning at 11:40. The magnetic lock logs no opening after 11:30. The calibration system reports a reset at 11:47. The corridor clock is later found running backwards. The printed access log contains one entry in a different typeface.

Break the 11:47 paradox

MASTER LOG OPENED

The timestamp was manufactured by manipulating the institute's synchronized clock calibration. The physical lock never opened; the event sequence was shifted in the records to make an ordinary movement look impossible. The decisive evidence is the calibration reset, not the corridor clock.
